Stallion Floor

This is how the worker app would look on a phone. Tap the Stallion icon: it opens full screen like any app, with no browser bars, and runs the real worker screen on preview data.

How a worker gets it

  1. Android: open the link once in Chrome → Install app. The horse icon goes on the home screen.
  2. iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. No app store, no updates to install: when the office PC is updated, every phone gets it next time the app opens.

Good to know

The full "install" button needs a secure link (https). That comes with the remote-access step (Tailscale or Cloudflare). Until then, phones on the office Wi-Fi can still add it to the home screen.

If the Wi-Fi drops, the app still opens and says it's waiting for signal. Nothing is saved on the phone except the screens.
